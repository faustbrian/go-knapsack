## BoxPacker common-process comparison

The separately recorded comparison used PHP 8.5.8, Go 1.27.1, BoxPacker
4.2.0, one warm-up, and ten alternating fresh processes per implementation.
Both implementations packed and independently verified the same two items in
one container. The Go adapter's p50 wall time was 3.300 ms and the BoxPacker
adapter's was 34.584 ms. These measurements describe one tiny common subset
and are not a general speed claim.
